A `Carousel` is a slideshow component for cycling through elements—images or slides of content—like a carousel. It allows users to browse through multiple items in a compact space.

## Component Usage

### Default

A default `Carousel` displays one item at a time with dot indicators for navigation. Users can click dots to jump to specific slides or swipe/drag to navigate.

<SCarouselDefault client:only="vue" />
<Code code={SCarouselDefaultRaw} lang="vue" title="default.vue" />

### Multi Item

A multi-item `Carousel` displays multiple items simultaneously. This example shows 3 items at once, perfect for product showcases or image galleries.

<SCarouselMultiItem client:only="vue" />
<Code code={SCarouselMultiItemRaw} lang="vue" title="multiItem.vue" />

### Autoplay

An autoplay `Carousel` automatically advances through slides at specified intervals. The autoplay pauses when users hover or focus on the carousel.

<SCarouselAutoplay client:only="vue" />
<Code code={SCarouselAutoplayRaw} lang="vue" title="autoplay.vue" />

### Vertical

A vertical `Carousel` scrolls items vertically instead of horizontally. Useful for displaying content in a vertical layout.

A `Carousel` with previous/next buttons positioned on the sides. When `loop` is disabled, buttons are automatically disabled at the start and end.
